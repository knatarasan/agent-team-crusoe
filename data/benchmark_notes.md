# Separate benchmark fixtures

The original `dataset.csv` is the complete 50-claim training set for the end-to-end experiment. Its content and extracted answer cache are unchanged by learning.

`validation.jsonl` and `hidden_test.jsonl` each contain 24 synthetic claims authored before the improvement run. Each has four examples for every route: fast-track, deny, coverage review, SIU, subrogation, and adjuster. Each record includes explicit structured gold, a rationale, and references to existing training examples establishing the demo convention. Gold is not generated by the model being evaluated.

These are new incidents and fact combinations. IDs and normalized descriptions do not overlap across datasets. The fixtures share the demo's scenario families; they are not independent real-world or out-of-domain evidence. They have not been reviewed by an insurance expert, and their conventions are not assertions about current insurance law.

Counterexamples include delivery activity with an applicable endorsement, a policy exclusion for someone who was not driving, missing signatures, missing cancellation notices, and liability-only policies. Confirmed cancellation cases explicitly establish the contractual grace period and mailed notice. Severe damage and ordinary injuries are separated from fraud patterns.

Gold uses the existing demo conventions: SIU takes precedence over the adjuster route; SIU is a referral, not itself a coverage denial; deductible_applies is false when the scenario's expected outcome includes eventual full recovery/refund, and true for partial-fault recovery. The selected demo fault positions for disputed reversing, failed brake lights, and suspected staging come from the existing dataset rather than new legal research.

The supervisor receives only training failures. Validation is used to select edits. The final playbook is frozen before either version is evaluated on the hidden test. Both fixture files are hashed in the experiment manifest and must not be edited based on run results.
